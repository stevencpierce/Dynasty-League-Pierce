Contracts & Salary Cap

The league runs a hard salary cap — a team may never exceed it, and any trade that would put a team over the cap is invalid. Auction free agents sign for 1, 2, 3, or 4 years, but long-term deals are capped per team.

2026 Amendment

The 2-year contract limit was increased from 3 to 6 players — giving teams room to lock in depth and rebuild core pieces without burning through their 3-year and 4-year slots.

How to spend your slots

  • 4-year deals — elite, long-term building blocks. You get exactly one, so use it wisely.
  • 3-year deals — second-tier assets and role players you want to lock in (you get two).
  • 2-year deals — depth and role players. As of 2026 you get six.
  • 1-year deals — flexibility and short-term needs, unlimited within your cap space.

Cutting a player may incur dead cap, charged in the season(s) of the cut.

02

Practice Squad

New in 2026. A 3-slot roster for stashing young, developing players during the season — free of cap and roster cost.

Slots
3 practice-squad spots. They don't count against your 25-man active roster or your salary cap — effectively free space for development.
Timing
In-season tool only. Players can be added only after the free-agent auction, during the season — never before or during the auction.
Eligibility
Only players with two full NFL seasons or less. A player entering their 3rd NFL season is eligible; entering their 4th is not. League-drafted rookies go straight to the active roster and can't be stashed here.
Contracts
No multi-year contracts — practice-squad players are in-season FAAB pickups.
Activation
Promote to the active roster anytime you have an open spot. One-way only: once activated, a player can't return to the practice squad.
Poaching
Other teams can poach your practice-squad players with FAAB bids. You get right of first refusal within the waiver window (currently 1 day): activate the player in time and the poach fails; otherwise the poaching team gets them.
03

Injured Reserve

Two flavors of IR, four slots total. Both free up a roster spot — but not cap space.

Standard IR · 2 slots

  • Player sits indefinitely until you move them off.
  • No automatic return date.
  • Use for season-ending or long-term injuries.

IR-DFR · 2 slots

  • Designated for return after exactly 6 weeks.
  • Auto-moves to your active roster when eligible — you must have space.
  • RSO tracks the 6-week clock and notifies you.

Cap impact: players on IR / IR-DFR still count against the salary cap. They free up a roster spot, not cap space.

05

Trades

Move players — with their contracts — and draft picks. The math has to work for both sides.

  • Trades may include players (with their existing contracts) and draft picks.
  • Both teams must remain cap-compliant after the trade — a deal that puts either team over the cap doesn't go through.
  • Trade deadline and any veto/review rules to be recorded here.
